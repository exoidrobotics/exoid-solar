Pricing & ROI

Cost + margin: price = equipment cost (panels, inverters, batteries at the cost price in the Equipment catalog) + materials + crew days × crew cost + other costs, then your margin on top (never less than the minimum profit), plus net metering. Crew days are estimated per job and can be overridden on the project page (⚙ Advanced). Built-in cost prices are market estimates; replace them with your supplier prices.

Itemized: price = kWp × the rate below (panels, mounting, PV and AC wiring, breakers, SPDs, labor) + each inverter's and battery's selling price from the Equipment catalog + net metering. Set your real selling prices there with Edit; the built-in prices are market estimates.

Pick the client's electric company on the Project & loads page to fill in the rate. The export credit is an estimate; check the net-metering credit on the client's bill. The net-metering fee is added to the price whenever net metering is switched on for a project.
